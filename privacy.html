Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

myKehila is a community application designed to help Jewish communities worldwide share information, events, announcements, Jewish resources, and community services with their members.

Information We Collect

myKehila does not require users to create an account or sign in.

The app does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address unless you voluntarily provide such information when contacting a community or using an external service.

Technical Information

myKehila uses third-party infrastructure and services, including Firebase services provided by Google, to operate certain app functionality.

These services may process limited technical information such as device identifiers, push notification tokens, app diagnostics, or other technical information necessary to provide and maintain the service.

Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, myKehila may use a device notification token to deliver community announcements, event updates, reminders, and other relevant notifications.

You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

Community Content

The app displays information provided by participating communities, including announcements, events, contact information, religious content, and community services.

Participating communities may provide links to external websites or services. myKehila is not responsible for the privacy practices of those external services.

Third-Party Services

myKehila may use third-party services including:

  • Google Firebase
  • Apple Push Notification Service (APNs)

These providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information is not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Children's Privacy

myKehila is a general community application and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as myKehila develops and new functionality is introduced. Changes will be published on this page.
