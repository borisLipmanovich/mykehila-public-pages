MyKehila Support

Welcome to MyKehila Support.

MyKehila is a community app designed for Jewish communities worldwide. It provides community announcements, events, Shabbat and candle-lighting times, Torah content, Jewish calendar information, prayers, learning resources, and useful community services.

Some content in MyKehila is provided directly by participating communities. If your question relates to a specific event, announcement, service, or community activity, please contact the relevant community using the contact information available inside the app.
